Welcome!

I love renewable energy, so much that I think about its whole life story.

It starts with a big question: how do we get renewables deployed at the scale we need for net zero? For me, the answer lies in policy and electricity market design. Markets weren't built for wind and solar, so rethinking how they work is key to letting renewables thrive.

But deployment isn't the end of the story. Solar panels eventually reach the end of their life, and we need to handle them sustainably too. I look at this through carbon accounting and circular economy perspectives.
