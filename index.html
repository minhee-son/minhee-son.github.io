Welcome! I love renewable energy — so much that I think about its whole life story.

It starts with a big question: how do we get renewables deployed at the scale we need for net zero? For me, the answer lies in policy and electricity market design. Markets weren't built for wind and solar, so rethinking how they work is key to letting renewables thrive.

But the story doesn't end at deployment. Solar panels retire too, and dealing with them sustainably matters just as much. That's where carbon accounting and the circular economy come in — they're the lenses that help me see the full picture, from the first kilowatt-hour to the last recycled panel.

I am a Research Fellow at the Energy Studies Institute, National University of Singapore.

research interests

Solar energy policy and end-of-life PV waste · carbon accounting (Scope 1/2/3, GHG Protocol, 24/7 CFE) · electricity market design for high renewable penetration · life-cycle assessment (LCA, DMFA, CBA) · energy transition technologies (hydrogen, CCUS, batteries).
