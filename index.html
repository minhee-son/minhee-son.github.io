I am a Research Fellow at the Energy Studies Institute, National University of Singapore. My research sits at the intersection of energy transition policy and quantitative environmental assessment — from end-of-life solar PV waste governance to Scope 3 carbon accounting for cloud computing and data centres.

I hold a PhD and a Master's degree in Energy and Environmental Policy from Korea University, and have around 14 years of experience in environmental impact analysis across Korea and Singapore. My current work spans international decarbonisation strategies, electricity market reform, and localised emission factors for ICT infrastructure.

research interests

Solar energy policy and end-of-life PV waste · carbon accounting (Scope 1/2/3, GHG Protocol, 24/7 CFE) · electricity market design for high renewable penetration · life-cycle assessment (LCA, DMFA, CBA) · energy transition technologies (hydrogen, CCUS, batteries).
